How I approach a project

The same three moves, whether the deliverable is a report, a dashboard, or a piece of software.

01 — Understand

Get inside the data first

Before touching code, I trace where the numbers come from, where they disagree, and what decision they're actually meant to support.

02 — Model

Build something that holds up

Clean schemas, tested pipelines, and metrics defined once and reused everywhere — so the answer doesn't change depending on who asks.

03 — Ship

Hand over something usable

A dashboard, an app, or an automated report — built for the person who'll open it every week, not just the one who asked for it.
